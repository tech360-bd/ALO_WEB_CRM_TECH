# Data Provenance & Verification Policy — ALO Education

## Provenance Standard
Every public university, course, fee, requirement, and ranking record must store:
- `source_name`
- `source_url`
- `content_hash`
- `collected_at`
- `last_verified_at`
- `verified_by`
- `verification_status` (`VERIFIED`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `NEEDS_UPDATE`, `QUARANTINED`, `REJECTED`)
