# ALO Education — Portal User Guide

## Student Portal
- **Login**: Click "Student Portal" in navbar → enter email/password
- **Dashboard**: View profile completion, applications, documents, timeline
- **Profile**: 4-step form (Personal, Education, Passport/Work/Sponsor, Visa/Admission)
- **Documents**: Upload passport, transcripts, English test, SOP, CV, bank statement
- **Applications**: Track application status from counseling to enrollment
- **Course Finder**: AI-powered eligibility scoring for 218 courses
- **AI Interview**: Practice credibility, pre-CAS, and visa interviews
- **English Tests**: Free IELTS/PTE/TOEFL mock tests (3 free for ALO students)

## Partner Portal
- **Login**: Click "Partner Portal" in navbar
- **Dashboard**: KPIs (students, applications, vouchers, commission, quota)
- **Users**: Manage up to 3 partner employees (request extra seats from ALO)
- **Marketing Studio**: AI content generation (15 images/month, 10 videos/month)
- **Vouchers**: Generate 3 English mock vouchers per eligible student
- **Benefits**: 6 benefit types per student with active ALO application

## Employee Portal
- **Login**: Click "Employee Portal" in navbar
- **Role-based dashboard**: Counselor, Application Team, Visa Officer, Marketing, Finance, HR
- **Privacy Mode**: Sensitive data (salary, bank) blurred by default, PIN to reveal
- **Attendance**: Clock in/out, leave requests, payroll
- **Communication**: Unified inbox (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, email, SMS)
